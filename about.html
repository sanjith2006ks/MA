Our Story & Culture

Discover how georgekuttybush evolved from an independent design concept into a vibrant youth movement.

Built for the Next Generation

Founded with the core goal of breaking away from monotonous fast fashion, georgekuttybush represents vibrant individuality, high contrast, and functional streetwear aesthetics.

We craft limited-run apparel using premium materials, high-energy palettes (signature orange and navy blue), and custom cuts tailored specifically for 16-to-25-year-old youth creators.

Our Mission

To empower young creators to express their identity boldly through durable, high-impact streetwear.

Our Vision

To establish a global lifestyle brand recognized for sustainable, limited-edition youth apparel and culture.

Our USP

High-contrast colorways, ethically sourced 400 GSM fabrics, and drops limited strictly to 500 units per design.
